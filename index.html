Static Bloom

Original Score

Built around a detuned piano loop recorded through a broken tape deck, layered with swelling string samples and a slow-building low end. Written for a scene about growth found in decay.

Low Tide, No Light

Ambient / Sound Design
5:18

Field recordings from an empty parking garage, stretched and pitched down, paired with a single sustained cello note. Made to sit underneath dialogue without ever announcing itself.

Produced byMiguel Andri Sound Design byMiguel Andri Mastered byLior Vance

Rust & Repeat

Production — feat. Marlow
2:57

A collaboration built from a single vocal take, chopped and re-pitched until the words blur into texture. The drums were recorded on a kit found in a storage unit.

Produced & Mixed byMiguel Andri Vocals byMarlow Mastered byLior Vance

Fault Lines (Reprise)
